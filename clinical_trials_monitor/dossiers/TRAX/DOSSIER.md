## 1. Executive Summary & Reading Guide

**First Tracks Biotherapeutics, Inc.** (Nasdaq: **TRAX**) is a San Diego–based,
clinical-stage biotechnology company developing next-generation antibody
therapeutics that modulate immune pathways implicated in autoimmune and
inflammatory disease. It is classified by the SEC under *Pharmaceutical
Preparations (SIC 2834)*, incorporated in Delaware, fiscal year ending 12/31.

First Tracks is a **2026 spin-off of AnaptysBio, Inc. (Nasdaq: ANAB)**. AnaptysBio
announced the intent to separate into two standalone public companies on
**September 29, 2025**; First Tracks' Form 10 registration statement was declared
effective by the SEC on **April 1, 2026**; and the separation completed on the
**April 20, 2026 Distribution Date**, with each AnaptysBio holder receiving one
TRAX share per ANAB share held at the record date. First Tracks carried the
clinical pipeline (rosnilimab, ANB033, ANB101) into the new entity; AnaptysBio
retained the GSK (Jemperli/dostarlimab) and Vanda financial-royalty
collaborations.

- **Capital position:** Launched with a multi-year cash position and a stated
  ~two-year runway; Q1 2026 net loss of **$50.5M** (vs $47.2M in Q1 2025); cash
  and investments reported around **$286.5M**, augmented by ~$100M transferred
  at spin and ~$80M from a private placement (figures vary by source and pre-
  vs post-separation cut — verify against the 10-Q in §3).
- **Clinical footprint:** Three antibody assets — lead **ANB033** (anti-CD122)
  in Phase 1b, **rosnilimab** (PD-1+ pathogenic-T-cell depleter) with positive
  Phase 2b RA data and FDA End-of-Phase-2 alignment, and **ANB101** (BDCA2
  modulator) in Phase 1a.
- **Disclosure cadence:** New registrant — Form 10 + amendments, an S-1 resale
  shelf, the first standalone 10-Q (Q1 2026), and spin-off 8-Ks are the primary
  documents to read first.
- **Former identity:** Founded 2019; formerly *Biopharma Co., Inc.*; renamed
  *First Tracks Biotherapeutics, Inc.* in February 2026. When-issued shares
  traded as **TRAXV** before regular-way **TRAX** trading.

**RENOIR Phase 2b headline (rosnilimab, RA):** 424 patients; mean baseline
DAS28-CRP 5.64, CDAI 37.7. Primary endpoint (Δ DAS28-CRP at Wk12) met by all
doses (−2.06 / −2.12 / −2.06 for 100/400/600 mg vs −1.69 placebo, p<0.01); ACR20
/ ACR50 / CDAI-LDA secondaries statistically significant; ~69% of treated
patients reached CDAI-LDA by Wk14 with sustained responses toward Wk28.
Pharmacodynamics: ~90% reduction of PD-1-high T cells, ~50% of PD-1+ T cells,
increased total Tregs, ~50% mean CRP reduction. Safety: no malignancy/MACE/
serious-infection signal vs placebo through Wk12.

  ANB033 Phase 1b celiac/EoE data and the rosnilimab Phase 3/partnership
  decision are the catalysts. Cross-reference §4 with 8-K data-readout filings.
- **Late-stage optionality without late-stage spend.** Rosnilimab has positive
  Phase 2b data and FDA EOP2 alignment but the company is explicitly seeking a
  partner / out-license / asset financing rather than self-funding Phase 3 —
  monitor 8-K material-agreement filings; a deal would materially reset the
  funding and value picture.
- **Lead-asset concentration.** ANB033 is the prioritised internal program;
  celiac and EoE Phase 1b outcomes (especially the Cohort 1 gluten-challenge
  mucosal-damage endpoint) are the single most important near-term data event.
- **Competitive context for ANB101.** The BDCA2/pDC space includes Biogen's
  litifilimab; First Tracks claims a more potent, longer-half-life profile —
  validate from Phase 1a pharmacodynamics when disclosed.
